Welcome to Golf Majors

Rules

  • Pick a team of 6 golfers for the 4 PGA majors
  • The Players Championship is optional
  • Sum the best 4 scores of the day for your golfers
  • In the event 4 golfers don't make the cut, 2 options
    • Player can be eliminated OR
    • The worst score of each day will count for each MC golfer
  • Best Team Golf Score at the end of the tournament is the winner
  • Season Winner can be tracked multiple ways
    • Best full season golf score
    • Most earned Money
    • Most Fedex Points
  • Optional bonus Points for 1st, 2nd, 3rd place golfers on your team
  • Several options for tiebreakers
