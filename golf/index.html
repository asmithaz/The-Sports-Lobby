Golf Challenges

FedEx Cup Playoffs
  • Draft a roster of golfers from the FedEx Cup Playoffs field of 70 before the FedEx St. Jude Championship begins.
  • Tiered draft option requires picks from set FedEx Cup ranking tiers (1-30, 31-50, 51-70), or choose Open Draft for no restrictions.
  • Score points based on each golfer's best finish across the St. Jude Championship, BMW Championship, and TOUR Championship.
  • Bonus points awarded if your roster includes the FedEx Cup Champion.
  • Live scoring updates throughout each tournament.
Majors Season
  • Men and Women's Major tournaments available.
  • Select a team of golfers for each Major Tournament including the Masters, U.S. Open, PGA Championship, and The Open.
  • Score Points based on golfer`s daily scores.
  • The Player`s Championship can be optionally added.
  • Customizable scoring options including bonus points, member groups, multiple entries, and more.
Full Season League
  • Each week league members select a golfer.
  • Players score points based on their golfer`s daily scores.
  • Players score points depending on golfer standings for each tournament.
  • Adaptable season length to meet your league`s needs.
  • Tournament winners are eliminated from selection the remainder of the season.
  • Customizable league options like weekly ties, winner revivals, bonus points for majors, in season bracket challenges, and many more.
Weekly Survivor Pick 'em
  • Select one golfer to make the cut each week.
  • Season length is customizable.
  • If they make the cut, move on, if not you are eliminated.
  • Each golfer can only be picked once per season.
In Season Tournament Bracket Challenge
  • Men and Women's tournaments available.
  • Players draft multiple golfers from a tournament to go head to head over the weekend.
  • If your golfer wins the head to head matchup move on and draft again the next week.
  • Typical tournament last 6 weeks and you can use preset options or choose the tournaments you`d like.
Money Accumulation Challenge
  • Select one or more golfers each week.
  • Accumulate golfer`s winnings for the week over the entire season.
  • Variable season lengths available.
  • Each player can only select each golfer once per season.
