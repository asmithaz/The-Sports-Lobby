3 Options to start
* Pick before the group stage only
* Pick before the knockout stage and skip the group stage
* Pick before the group stage, and repick the knockout stage

Increasing point structure

Option 1
Start from Group Stage and only get the ones you selected correctly in the bracket
Correct Winner - 20pts
Second Place - 10pts
Next 8 - 5 pts

Round of 32 - 40pts
Round of 16 - 80pts
Quarterfinals -  160pts
Semifinals - 320pts
Finals -640pts

Option 2
Knockout stage bracket without picking group stage
Round of 32 - 10pts
Round of 16 - 20pts
Quarterfinals -  40pts
Semifinals - 80pts
Finals -160pts

Option 3
Start from Group Stage and everyone gets to pick the entire knockout stage bracket, scores are cumulative
Correct Winner - 20pts
Second Place - 10pts
Next 8 - 5 pts

Round of 32 - 10pts
Round of 16 - 20pts
Quarterfinals -  40pts
Semifinals - 80pts
Finals -160pts
