Pro Football Challenges

Set it, Forget it League
  • Teams are selected in a snake or auction draft.
  • Teams go head to head each week.
  • No roster changes, no waiver pick ups, no trades! One substitution allowed midway through the season.
  • Huge team rosters for high scoring weekly games.
  • Customizable scoring settings including rushing, receiving, and passing yards, touchdowns, kicking yards, etc.
NFL Playoffs Bracket
  • Player's select winner for every game in the bracket.
  • Points awarded for every correct pick.
  • Customizable scoring settings including point values per round, multiple entries, member groupings, confidence rankings and more.
  • Real time standings report and what if scenarios.
NFL Survivor
  • Pick one team to win their matchup from a preset pool of games.
  • Players with winning picks move on to the next week until all teams are eliminated.
  • Playoff specific survivor game available for bowl season.
  • Each team can only be picked once per season.
NFL Pick 'em
  • Pick every game winner each week.
  • Available to play against the spread or straight up.
  • Customizable scoring including confidence ranking.
Gauntlet Playoff Tournament
  • Each player is randomly assigned one team or more (up to 14 entries).
  • Player's with losing teams move on while losing teams are eliminated.
Playoff Draft
  • Teams are selected in a snake or auction draft.
  • Players keep the teams that are drafted through the playoffs and accumulate points.
  • Customizable scoring settings including point values per round, multiple entries, member groupings, confidence rankings and more.
Playoff Weekly Strategy Game
  • Player's draft a team based on teams available in the playoffs.
  • Points awarded based on players achievements in game, just like fantasy football!
  • Multipliers awarded for taking the same players each week.
  • Points awarded are cumulative.
  • Customizable scoring settings including rushing, receiving, and passing yards, touchdowns, kicking yards, etc.
Kicker League
  • All Kicker Teams go head to head each week.
  • Customizable scoring settings including PATs, made and missed field goals, distance of attempts, etc.
  • Leagues last through the regular season.
