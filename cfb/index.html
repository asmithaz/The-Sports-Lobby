College Football Challenges

Weekly Pick 'em
  • Select winners of multiple games each week.
  • Groups play against the spread or straight up.
  • Points awarded for every correct pick.
  • Choose only the conferences you want or choose from our default groupings.
  • Customizable scoring settings including point values per game, confidence rankings and more.
  • Real time standings reports and what if scenarios.
Bowl Season Pick 'em
  • Select the winner of every bowl game during college bowl season.
  • Available to play against the spread or straight up.
  • Points awarded for every correct pick.
  • Customizable scoring settings including confidence rankings, bonus points for college football playoff rounds, and more.
  • Real time standings report and what if scenarios.
College Survivor
  • Pick one team to win their matchup from a preset pool of games.
  • Players with winning picks move on to the next week until all teams are eliminated.
  • Conference specific survivor games available.
  • Each team can only be picked once per season.
