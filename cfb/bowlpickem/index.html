Welcome to College Football Bowl Pick'em

Rules

  • Pick every single bowl game against the spread or straight up
  • Spreads lock before the first bowl game
  • CFP quarterfinals, semifinals and championship spreads lock 2 days before game
  • Change your pick up until 5 minutes before scheduled game time
  • Each correct non-College Football Playoff pick is worth one point
  • Each correct College Football Playoff round is worth the following

    • Round Points per Correct Pick Total Points Possible
    Round 1 1 4
    Quarterfinals 2 8
    Semifinals 3 6
    Championship 4 4

  • Highest Total Score after CFP championship is the winner
  • Tiebreaker is the closest guess for the CFP championship total score
