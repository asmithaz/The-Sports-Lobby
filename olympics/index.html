Olympic Challenges

Country Draft Challenge
  • Draft countries in an auction or snake draft.
  • Points awarded based on medals won by athletes from drafted countries.
  • Cumulative scores determine the winner.

Medal Pick 'em
  • Select Winning Countries for every event, pre set event lists, or custom event lists.
  • Assign gold, silver, and bronze to the country of your choosing.
  • Every correct pick scores points!
  • Customizable scoring settings including point values per medal, multiple entries, member groupings, confidence rankings and more.
